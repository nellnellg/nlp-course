A game about how computers read

Teach Beep to read

Beep is a robot who cannot read yet. To a computer, words are just one long line of letters. Help Beep read a runner's note, one step at a time.

    Cut it into pieces

    Beep sees one long line of letters. First we cut it into sentences. Then we cut each sentence into words. Each little piece has a grown up name, a token.

    Guess first

    The note says the runner wants to finish in 3:25. Does Beep cut 3:25 into one piece or three?

    Did you see?
    • 3:25 stayed in one piece, because it is a time.
    • I'm got cut into I and 'm.

    Skip the boring words

    Words like the, am and to show up in almost every sentence. They do not tell Beep what the note is about, so we skip them. We skip the dots too.

    Guess first

    All four words are in the note. One of them sneaks past. Which one?

    Did you see?
    • I sneaked past because it starts with a capital letter. Beep's list of boring words is all lowercase.

    Chop off the endings

    Run and running mean almost the same thing. Chopping the ends off words makes them match. It is fast, but a little messy.

    Guess first

    What does Beep turn average into?

    Did you see?
    • running became run, so now it matches run.
    • average became averag. That is not a real word, and Beep does not mind.

    Look it up in the dictionary

    Chopping can make fake words. This time Beep looks each word up in a dictionary, so it always gets a real word back. But Beep needs a hint about what kind of word it is.

    Guess first

    Beep looks up training with no hint. What does it get back?

    Did you see?
    • With no hint, Beep thinks every word is a thing, like "a training". Tell Beep it is an action, and training becomes train.

    Give each word a job

    Some words are names. Some are actions. Some are numbers. Beep puts a sticker on each word to show what job it does.

    Guess first

    In "will get me there", get is an action. Which sticker does Beep give it?

    Did you see?
    • Beep got get wrong. We skipped "will" and "me" in step 2, so Beep could not tell it was an action.

    Find the important names

    Last step! Beep looks for names of people, places and groups. It glues the name words together and gives each name a label.

    Guess first

    What does Beep think the Miami Marathon is?

    Did you see?
    • Beep found Coach Kale. But it thinks the Miami Marathon is a person! Beep is still learning.

    The finish line

    You taught Beep six steps. Each step uses what the step before it made.

    Step 1

    Cut into pieces

    One long line becomes 48 pieces.

    Step 2

    Skip boring words

    48 pieces become 27.

    Step 3

    Chop the endings

    running becomes run.

    Step 4

    Look it up

    miles becomes mile.

    Step 5

    Give each word a job

    Kale gets a name sticker.

    Step 6

    Find the names

    Coach Kale is a person.

    Beep still does not know what a marathon is. It just has neat pieces with labels. Next, Beep will learn to turn those pieces into numbers.
